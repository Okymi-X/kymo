# Sample data

`kymo-server seed-demo` (or the Load sample tournaments button on the home page, as an organizer) fills a workspace with data for every feature, built through the same functions the API uses. It is safe to run again: events that exist are left alone.

## Accounts

Every sample account shares **one password, generated by each run**. The command prints it with the list of accounts; the button shows it on the page. Running the seed again makes a new password and applies it to all of them, so share the newest one.

| Account | Role | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| `demo-admin` | admin | Manage events, permissions, users, participants |
| `demo-referee` | referee | Record results |
| `demo-monitor` | monitor | Read traffic, bans and the audit log |
| `demo-ada`, `demo-linus`, ... (16 players) | player | Play. The first twelve are in teams of three: Red, Blue, Green and Gold Team |
| `demo-newbie` | player | Has no events: join one |
| `demo-late` | player | Added to Forensics Weekend by an organizer after registration closed |
| `demo-removed` | player | Was removed from an event (see the audit log) |

## What is in it

No challenge needs a container or a lab image.

**Jeopardy events**
- *Forensics Weekend* (solo, live): the public board is frozen as of 90 minutes ago while staff see it moving. Single flags (fixed and dynamic points, hints) and three multi-question challenges with files; players have answered some questions and not others.
- *Team Forensics Cup* (teams, live): teammates answer for the same team.
- *Early Bird CTF*: started ahead of its schedule; registration closes at a set time.
- *Paused Practice*: paused after a few solves.
- *Closed Registration Cup*: an organizer closed registration by hand.
- *Autumn Forensics*: finished, with multi-question challenges, the final board and the score over time.
- Plus the original *Spring*, *Winter* and *Summer* Jeopardy events.

**Tournaments decided by challenges** (the Play tab, first among the tournament tabs, for the players in them)
- *Duel Cup* (solo, single elimination, drawn per match): one match tied and broken by who finished first, one closed by an organizer, one in play, one not started.
- *Team Duel Cup* (teams, drawn per round, 1 hour limit): a decided match and one in play with a countdown.
- *Challenge League* (round robin, picked by hand): a draw, a win, and matches with no challenges yet.
- Plus the original tournaments with typed-in scores, in every format.

**Also seeded:** solves and answers spread over many hours (so the score chart, ladder history and activity feeds have shape), wrong guesses, an organizer adding and removing participants (audit log), and every lifecycle state.
